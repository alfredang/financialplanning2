    Good questions

    Things people often ask

    Is the first chat really free?

    Yep! The first 30-minute chat is free with no strings attached. If we're not the right fit, we'll happily point you somewhere that is.

    Do I need lots of money to work with you?

    Not at all. Some of our favourite clients started with a fresh-grad salary and a dream. Good planning matters most when you're just starting out.

    Will you try to sell me products?

    Our job is to build a plan that works for you. We explain every recommendation and how we're paid, in plain English, and you decide. No pressure, ever.

    What should I bring to the first chat?

    Just yourself and your questions. A rough idea of your income, savings and any policies you hold helps, but we can figure the details out together.

    How do you keep my information safe?

    We only ask for what we need, never ask for passwords or bank PINs, and we'll never share your details with third parties for marketing.
